Step 6 · ADHD Boosters
The good stuff.
Evidence-backed tweaks that make AI output actually work for your brain. Toggle what helps.
🧩 Break into chunks
Small sections, not walls of text. Working memory can only hold 3–4 things at once.
📊 Use tables where possible
Scan a table in seconds. Read a paragraph in never.
**Bold the key terms**
Visual anchors guide attention. 103-study meta-analysis says so.
💡 Give a concrete example
Abstract explanations bounce off ADHD brains. Examples stick.
🎯 Lead with the point
No preamble. No "great question!" Just the answer, first.
✅ End with one next action
One thing to do. Not five. The initiation freeze is real.
⏱️ Add time estimates
ADHD time blindness is documented. Estimates help.
🚫 No jargon
Plain language only. Jargon burns cognitive load you don't have spare.
🔁 Offer a simpler version
Ask the AI to offer an even simpler version at the end.
